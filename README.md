AI-Powered Bid Response Engine: Smart RFP & Tender Automation	 

To automate the enterprise bid and tender process using AI and RAG, reducing manual proposal drafting efforts by up to 50%. By building an intelligent system that automatically extracts requirements from complex RFPs and matches them with internal company data, we empower teams to draft winning proposals efficiently and accurately.	 

 

"Large enterprises often handle over 120 bids (RFPs/Tenders) annually, with each document spanning anywhere from 50 to 500 pages. Bid managers waste 60-80% of their time manually reading through these massive documents, isolating compliance clauses, and hunting down past project references or relevant employee CVs. This manual process is not only incredibly stressful and slow but also highly prone to human error. 

  

The most significant risk in this workflow is that missing even a minor compliance requirement can lead to instant disqualification, resulting in a direct loss of potential revenue. Gathering data across siloed departments to draft a cohesive, tailored proposal adds massive overhead, delaying the critical Go/No-Go decision-making process. 

  

The goal of this hackathon challenge is to build a Bid Response Engine leveraging LLMs and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation). This engine will parse dense documents, extract critical criteria, and map them against a company capability library (past proposals, certifications, CVs) to generate an accurate first draft. This solution will drastically reduce human error, save hundreds of hours, and streamline the entire bidding lifecycle."	 

 

"Smart Document Extraction: Automatically extract mandatory requirements, deadlines, and Q&A formats from dense PDF or DOCX files. 

Capability Matching: Map RFP requirements to internal capability library such as past projects, CVs, and certifications. 

Gap Analysis & Compliance Checklist: Highlight missing capabilities as red flags. 

Auto-Drafting with Grounded RAG: Draft RFP answers using exact evidence from internal data. 

Predictive Win Scoring: Generate a Win-Probability score to support Go/No-Go decisions."	 

 

"RFP Ingestion & Extraction Pipeline: Upload and parse RFP structure, requirements, and deadlines. 

Compliance Checklist Dashboard: Show extracted requirements with Pass, Partial, or Fail status. 

Grounded Answer Drafter: Draft proposal sections with cited reference sources. 

Human-in-the-Loop Review UI: Review, edit, refine, approve, and export AI-generated drafts."
